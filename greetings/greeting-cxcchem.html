Introduction

Hello! My name is Kady James. I am a teacher with a passion for education and helping students succeed. This website is a platform where I share resources, teaching tips, and insights about my journey in the field of education.

Our course offers the following...

  • In depth PowerPoint (ppt/pptx), PDF or word document class notes / hand outs of the topics covered subdivided into sections
  • Pre-recorded videos of topics with dynamic content, that helps to visualize chemical reactions, bringing ideas to life!
  • Critical thinking worksheets that tie together content taught on our platform as well as past paper questions
  • Hand made summary sheets of each topic for quick reviews
  • Step-by-step instructions detailing organic reactions
  • 24 hour response time for questions, queries and issues
  • Past papers of at least 5 years or more, with answer sheets post submission of student attempts
