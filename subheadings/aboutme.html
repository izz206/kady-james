Hello, I am Dr James and this site is dedicated to helping students with writing the CSEC and CAPE examinations in the areas of Biology, Chemistry, Mathematics and Physics.

The site includes class notes, interactive videos explaining various concepts, test-yourself-worksheets/summary sheets and past paper assistance as well as live class teaching sessions. There is also a live message center where students can send queries asking for further explanation.

After receiving an additional national scholarship recipient and went on to the University of the West Indies to pursue a Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery degree; my interests have always been in teaching and has been doing so after graduating from U.W.I. I started off as private tutoring where students excelled with Grades Is & IIs. Her knowledge is not limited to lecturing as she also has laboratory experience through working as a teacher at both the CSEC and CAPE levels.

My goal is to assist STEM students in achieving their academic pursuits and get the dream careers they wish to achieve.
