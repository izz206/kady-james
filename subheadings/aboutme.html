Get to know more about me

Hello, My name is Dr. Crystal James and this platform was designed with the goal of aiding STEM students in becoming more successful.

We offer a collection of notes, media, and testing resources along with live classes.

After graduating from the University of the West Indies as a qualified medical physician I started off doing private lessons in Biology and Mathematics

My passion for teaching and educating became further ignited and I wanted to create a platform where students could receive quality classes and content in an easy-to-follow way

My goal is to assist STEM students in achieving their academic pursuits and get the dream careers they wish to achieve.
